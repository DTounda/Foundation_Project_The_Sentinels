MediCheck – User Consent

1. Consent to Essential Data Processing

When you create an account or use MediCheck, you agree that the application can process information needed to provide its services.

This may include:

Account management information: Your full name and email address.

Allergy information: Information about your allergies to identify possible ingredient-related warnings.

Medicine verification records: Records that support the medicine verification service.

Medicine photos: Photos submitted for processing through the OCR service.

Reports: Reports and other information you choose to provide.

This information is processed to provide the main features of MediCheck, as explained in the Privacy Notice.

2. Optional Pilot Research and Analytics Consent

MediCheck may use information about how the application is used to evaluate and improve the application during the pilot.

This may include general usage patterns, verification activity and application errors.

This consent is optional.

Please select one:

I agree to the use of my application-usage information for optional pilot research and analytics.

I do not agree to the use of my application-usage information for optional pilot research and analytics.

3. Your Choice

You can decline optional pilot research and analytics.

Choosing not to give optional consent should not prevent you from accessing essential medicine verification features.

If you wish to withdraw your optional consent, please contact the MediCheck team through the privacy contact.

Your information will be handled according to the MediCheck Privacy Notice.

4. Acknowledgement

I confirm that I have read and understood the MediCheck Privacy Notice.
