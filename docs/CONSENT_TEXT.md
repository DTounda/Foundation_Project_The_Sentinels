# MediCheck – User Consent

## 1. Consent to Essential Data Processing

When you create an account or use MediCheck, you agree that the application can process information needed to provide its services.

This may include:

- **Account management information:** Your full name, email address, and phone number.
- **Doctor and pharmacy information:** Doctor and pharmacy details that you provide.
- **Health information:** Your allergy information, allergy severity, and current medications.
- **Medicine verification records:** Records that support the medicine verification service, including verification methods, results, and allergy-warning status.
- **Medicine photos:** Photos submitted for processing through the OCR service to help recognise medicine names.
- **Reports:** Reports and other information you choose to provide.

This information is processed to provide the main features of MediCheck, as explained in the Privacy Notice.

## 2. Account Deletion

You can delete your MediCheck account and associated data through the Profile screen in the application.
